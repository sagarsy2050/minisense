# GreenLeaf Bistro — Customer Experience FAQ

## Menu & Food Quality

**Q: What are your most popular menu items?**
A: Our top sellers are the Avocado Toast, the Garden Bowl, and the Cold
Brew Coffee. Seasonal specials rotate every six weeks and are announced on
our app and printed table cards.

**Q: Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?**
A: Yes. Every item on the menu is labeled for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free,
and nut-free options. Kitchen staff are trained on cross-contamination
procedures for severe allergies, and guests can request a manager
consultation before ordering.

**Q: How fresh are your ingredients?**
A: Produce is sourced from local farms three times a week. Anything not
sold by closing on delivery day is donated to a community food bank rather
than held over, which is part of why menu availability can vary slightly
day to day.

## Wait Times & Service

**Q: What is your average wait time?**
A: We target under 10 minutes for counter orders during off-peak hours.
Peak hours (12–1 PM and 6–8 PM) may see 15–20 minute waits, especially on
weekends. Mobile pre-orders skip the counter line entirely.

**Q: Can I make a reservation?**
A: Reservations are accepted for parties of 6 or more, up to 30 days in
advance. Smaller parties are seated first-come, first-served, with a
digital waitlist visible from the entrance.

**Q: How do you handle complaints?**
A: All complaints are escalated to the shift manager within 15 minutes of
being raised. Refunds or replacements are offered for quality issues, and
recurring complaint categories are reviewed weekly by the operations team
so systemic problems (not just one-off mistakes) get fixed.

## Quality & Satisfaction Targets

**Q: What is your CSAT target?**
A: We aim for a CSAT of 4.5+ (share of survey responses rated 4 or 5 out of
5). Scores below 4.0 in any rolling 30-day window trigger a root-cause
review with the operations team, covering staffing levels, ticket times,
and ingredient quality.

**Q: How do you use customer survey feedback?**
A: Every guest receives a short survey link after checkout. Ratings and
free-text comments feed into a weekly operations dashboard. Themes that
appear in more than 5% of a location's responses in a given week are
flagged for manager review, and location-level trends are compared
month-over-month to catch slow drift before it becomes a pattern.

## Staffing & Training

**Q: How are staff trained on customer experience?**
A: New hires complete a two-week onboarding that includes shadowing shifts,
a customer-communication module, and a food-safety certification. Ongoing
coaching is driven partly by survey themes — if "staff" complaints spike at
a location, that location gets a refresher session within the week.

## Pricing & Value

**Q: How do you set menu pricing?**
A: Pricing is reviewed quarterly against ingredient cost changes and local
market rates. We aim to keep core items (Avocado Toast, Garden Bowl) within
a stable price band year over year even when specials fluctuate, since
"value" is one of the most common themes in customer feedback.

## Locations & Hours

**Q: What are your hours?**
A: Most locations are open 7 AM–9 PM daily. A few downtown locations extend
to 10 PM on Fridays and Saturdays. Exact hours are listed per-location in
the app.

**Q: Do you operate multiple locations?**
A: Yes. GreenLeaf Bistro operates as a small multi-location business, and
each location is tracked separately in our survey system (by
`business_id`) so operations can compare performance across sites rather
than only in aggregate.
